# Flagship Aruba knowledge base

Source: https://www.flagshiparuba.com

## About
Flagship Aruba delivers a memorable mix of adventure, snorkeling, and
laid-back fun aboard a charming schooner. Tours depart from the pier in front
of MooMba Beach Bar on Palm Beach, Aruba.

## Boat tours
- Morning Snorkel Tour: 9am-1pm (4 hours), from $98 per adult. Three
  snorkel stops (Boca Catalina, SS Antilla shipwreck, Malmok Beach), snorkel
  gear included, unlimited soft drinks/juices/water, Caribbean snacks, live
  music. Arrive 30 minutes before departure.
- Afternoon Snorkel Tour: 1:30pm-5pm (3.5 hours), from $86 per adult. Two
  premium snorkel stops chosen daily by the captain (usually Boca Catalina
  and Malmok), snorkel gear included, Caribbean-style lunch, open bar (soft
  drinks, warm drinks, juices, cocktails), rope swing, island music. Arrive
  30 minutes before departure.
- Sunset Cruise: 5:30pm-7:30pm (2 hours), from $90 per adult. Open
  bar (soft drinks, juices, cocktails, snacks), Caribbean-inspired food, two
  swimming stops, music, and stargazing as the sky darkens. Suitable for
  couples, honeymooners, families and friends.
- Private charters are available for any group size, with custom itineraries
  and open bar included. Contact the crew for availability and pricing.

## Snorkel sites
- Boca Catalina: a calm, shallow bay with crystal-clear water, perfect for
  beginners and experienced snorkelers alike.
- Malmok Reef: vibrant coral formations and abundant marine life along the
  northwest coast.
- SS Antilla Shipwreck: a WWII-era German freighter, one of the largest
  shipwrecks in the Caribbean, home to colorful coral and tropical fish.

## FAQ
Q: What should I bring on the boat tour?
A: Sunscreen, a towel, swimwear, and a sense of adventure. Snorkeling gear,
drinks, and snacks are all provided.

Q: Is the tour suitable for beginners?
A: Absolutely. Most guests are first-timers who receive full equipment,
instruction, and patient in-water guidance from the crew.

Q: Do the boats have bathrooms onboard?
A: Yes, the schooner has an onboard bathroom.

Q: How long are the tours?
A: The Morning Snorkel Tour runs 4 hours, the Afternoon Snorkel Tour
runs 3.5 hours, and the Sunset Cruise runs 2 hours.

Q: Is there an open bar?
A: Yes, the Afternoon Snorkel Tour and Sunset Cruise include an open
bar with local drinks, beer, wine, and soft drinks.

Q: Can I book a private tour?
A: Yes, private charters can be customized to a group's preferences. Contact
the crew for availability and pricing.

Q: Are there strong currents at the snorkeling sites?
A: Aruba's snorkeling reefs offer calm, beginner-friendly conditions with
mild currents along the protected northwest coast.

Q: How do tides affect snorkeling conditions?
A: Aruba's mild tidal range keeps conditions consistently good, and the crew
picks the best times and spots for visibility.

Q: How do I see pricing or book?
A: Starting prices are listed on each tour page. Contact Flagship Aruba by
phone, WhatsApp or email to confirm exact pricing and availability.
